Privacy Policy

Effective: June 21, 2026 · Last updated: June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Voltresso collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. We've tried to write it in plain language. If anything is unclear, email us at voltresso@zohomail.eu.

1. Who we are

Voltresso is a coffee brewing companion app and website operated by Tarek Sayed, an independent developer based in Serbia. Voltresso is not part of any company or corporation; it is operated as an individual sole developer project.

If you need to contact us about anything in this policy, including to exercise any of the rights described below, email voltresso@zohomail.eu. Postal correspondence address is available on written request to the same email.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • The Voltresso mobile app, available on Android (with iOS planned for a future release)
  • The Voltresso marketing website at voltresso.vercel.app
  • The policy site you are currently reading at tarekn98.github.io/voltresso-privacy

3. What we collect and why

3.1 Account information

When you create a Voltresso account, we collect:

  • Email address — used as your account identifier and for password reset
  • Password — securely hashed by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); we never see, store, or log your raw password
  • Display name — what you choose to be called in the app
  • Avatar — either a preset avatar or a photo you upload (see Section 3.5 below)

If you sign in with Google, we additionally receive from Google your Google account identifier, name, and profile photo URL. We do not receive your Google password or access any other Google data.

3.2 Brewing data

To deliver Voltresso's core function — helping you brew better coffee — we store the brewing data you create in the app:

  • Brew logs: dose, yield, brew time, temperature, grind setting, pressure, tasting notes, rating, score, and which machine/bean you used
  • Custom recipes you create
  • Saved recipes and saved beans (your bookmarks)
  • Viewed beans (so we can show your recent activity)
  • User settings: units (Celsius/Fahrenheit, grams/oz, bar/psi), haptics preferences, reduced motion, analytics opt-in, notification preferences

This data is synced to our secure database so you can access your brewing history from a fresh install or new device.

3.3 Local-only data

Some data stays only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your selected espresso machine and grinder choices
  • Brewing streak counters, XP, level progress, and achievement-fired flags
  • Volt chat history (conversations with Volt, our in-app brewing companion)
  • Scanned coffee bag history (brand, origin, roast date — see Section 3.6)
  • Volt Local's learning state (goals, memory, recent topics)

This data is cleared if you uninstall the app or sign out.

3.4 Volt AI data

Voltresso includes an in-app brewing companion called Volt, available in two tiers:

Volt Local (free, default): Runs entirely on your device. It does not send any data to any server. Your conversations with Volt Local never leave your phone.

Volt Pro (paid, not yet active — planned for a future release): When activated, Volt Pro routes your messages through our Supabase Edge Function proxy to Anthropic's Claude API. The messages we send to Anthropic include:

  • Your message text
  • Context about your selected machine and grinder (model and brewing targets)
  • Your last 5 brew logs (dose, yield, time, temperature, computed score, your rating)
  • Your barista rank and streak
  • IDs of recipes you've saved
  • Freshness information from coffee bags you've scanned
  • Recent topics Volt has discussed with you (memory)

We do not send your email address, name, account ID, or any other directly-identifying personal information to Anthropic.

Volt Pro chat transcripts are not stored on Voltresso's servers. They live only on your device. Anthropic, under their standard API terms, may retain transcripts for up to 30 days for trust and safety review purposes; their retention policy is documented at anthropic.com/legal.

We do track aggregate usage counts (messages per month) on our servers solely to enforce monthly limits — these counters store no message content.

3.5 Camera and photos

Voltresso accesses your camera and photo library for two purposes:

  • Avatar upload: When you choose to upload a profile picture, the photo is uploaded to our Supabase Storage and stored at a path tied to your user ID. The avatar storage bucket is publicly readable (anyone with the exact URL can fetch the image), so you should treat your uploaded avatar as content you are comfortable being potentially shareable.
  • Bag barcode scanning: When you scan a coffee bag barcode, your device camera reads the barcode number. No photo is taken, stored, or uploaded — only the decoded barcode number leaves your device. The barcode is sent to the open-data project Open Food Facts (operated by a French non-profit) to look up product information.

3.6 Search queries

When the free tier of Volt performs a web search for product or coffee information, your search query is sent to Google Custom Search (Google's search API). The search query itself and the requesting IP address are visible to Google. We do not send your account information to Google.

3.7 Device and usage data

When you have analytics enabled (you can opt out at any time in Settings):

  • PostHog (our analytics provider) automatically receives anonymous data about device model, operating system version, app version, locale, timezone, screen size, network type, and an anonymous device identifier. PostHog also receives the names of events that occur in the app (e.g., "sign_up", "brew_logged", "recipe_saved") with minimal context.
  • We do not attach your email, name, or account ID to PostHog events. Events are tied only to an anonymous device-level identifier.

For diagnostic purposes (always-on, used to fix bugs):

  • Sentry (our error monitoring provider) automatically receives error details, device model, OS version, app release version, and approximately 20% of performance traces. Sensitive information (emails, authentication tokens, Supabase API keys, password fields) is automatically scrubbed before sending. Sentry may record the IP address from which an error report originated as part of its standard request handling.
  • We do not attach your account identity to Sentry events.

3.8 Network metadata

Like any internet service, our servers see your IP address when you make a request. We use IP addresses for rate-limiting (preventing abuse) in a short-lived counter table that is automatically purged hourly. IP addresses are not used to identify or track individual users beyond this immediate security purpose.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the EU, UK, or another GDPR-applicable jurisdiction, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Voltresso service you signed up for (account, brewing data sync, in-app features)
  • Legitimate interests — to monitor security, prevent abuse, fix bugs (Sentry error monitoring), and improve the product (analytics, where you have not opted out)
  • Consent — for optional analytics (you can opt in or out at any time in app Settings), for marketing or feedback emails if we ever send any, and for Volt Pro processing (opt-in via subscription)
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws and respond to valid legal requests

6. International data transfers

Voltresso's primary user data (your account, brewing data, custom recipes, saved beans, settings) is stored in the European Union (Ireland) on Supabase infrastructure.

Some of our service providers are located outside the EU (primarily the United States). When we transfer data to these providers — for example, error reports to Sentry, analytics events to PostHog, AI processing to Anthropic — we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), which are included in our agreements with these providers.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your Voltresso account is active. When you delete your account (see Section 10 below), we delete the data we control as described in this policy.

Specific retention details:

  • Brew logs, recipes, saved items, settings, profile: kept while your account exists; deleted on account deletion
  • Avatar images: deleted on account deletion (best-effort across all stored variants)
  • IP rate-limiting records: auto-purged hourly
  • Aggregate Volt usage counters (no message content): retained for billing-period accounting

8. AI processing disclosure

Voltresso uses artificial intelligence in two distinct ways:

Volt Local (free tier): A deterministic, on-device assistant. It does not call any AI API. Your conversations with Volt Local never leave your device.

Volt Pro (paid tier — not yet live): When activated, Volt Pro uses Claude (Anthropic's large language model). The data sent to Anthropic is described in Section 3.4 above. Volt Pro's output is intended as brewing advice and educational content. It is not infallible. Do not rely on Volt's responses for medical, legal, financial, or other consequential decisions.

You can opt out of Volt Pro at any time by not subscribing, by canceling your subscription, or by deleting your account. The free tier of Volt (Volt Local) does not involve any external AI service.

9. Children

Voltresso is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please email voltresso@zohomail.eu and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

10.1 Universal rights

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Access your data — much of it is visible to you directly in the app; for anything else, email voltresso@zohomail.eu
  • Correct inaccurate data — update your profile, brew logs, recipes, and settings directly in the app, or email us
  • Delete your account and the data we control — use the Delete Account option in Settings, or email us
  • Export a copy of your data — email us and we'll provide it in a standard format
  • Opt out of analytics — toggle Analytics off in Settings

10.2 Account deletion: scope

When you delete your account, we delete:

  • Your authentication record
  • Your profile
  • All brew logs, custom recipes, saved recipes, saved beans, viewed beans, and user settings
  • Your avatar image (across all formats)
  • Aggregate Volt usage counters tied to your account

Account deletion is effective immediately on our systems.

Important: third-party processor data. Some data held by third-party processors is not automatically erased by deleting your Voltresso account, because we cannot directly delete records inside their systems:

  • PostHog retains anonymous analytics events per their retention policy
  • Sentry retains anonymized error events per their retention policy
  • Anthropic may retain Volt Pro transcripts for up to 30 days per their standard API terms
  • Google Play / RevenueCat retain billing and entitlement records per their policies (when Pro launches)

If you want us to also request erasure from these processors on your behalf, email voltresso@zohomail.eu with the subject "Full Erasure Request" and we will initiate the corresponding requests within 30 days.

10.3 EU/UK rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or UK, in addition to the above you have the right to:

  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email voltresso@zohomail.eu.

10.4 California rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights including the right to know, the right to delete, the right to correct, the right to opt-out of "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (we do not sell or share for cross-context behavioral advertising — see Section 5), the right to limit use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect sensitive personal information as defined under CCPA), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

Submit a verifiable consumer request by emailing voltresso@zohomail.eu with the subject "California Privacy Request".

10.5 Other US state rights

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, and Virginia have rights under their respective state privacy laws (CPA, CTDPA, DPDPA, FDBR, ICDPA, KCDPA, MODPA, MCDPA, NDPA, NHPA, NJDPA, OCPA, RIDTPPA, TIPA, TDPSA, UCPA, VCDPA). These generally include rights to access, delete, correct, and opt-out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, email voltresso@zohomail.eu.

10.6 Canada rights (PIPEDA / Quebec Law 25)

Canadian residents have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of their personal information. Quebec residents have additional rights including the right to data portability and the right to be informed of automated decision-making. To exercise these rights, email voltresso@zohomail.eu.

11. Cookies and tracking on our website

The marketing website at voltresso.vercel.app uses a consent banner (provided by Termly) to manage cookies. Essential cookies (for site functionality and consent preferences) are always on. Any optional cookies require your explicit consent, which you can change at any time via the "Consent Preferences" link in the website footer.

The mobile app does not use cookies in the traditional sense. App-level preferences (whether you've completed onboarding, your settings choices, your local cache of brewing data) are stored using your device's standard local storage and remain on your device unless you sign out or delete the app.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encryption at rest for our primary database (Supabase)
  • Row-level security policies so each user can only access their own data
  • JWT-based authentication with automatic token rotation
  • Per-user rate limiting on sensitive operations (avatar upload, account deletion)
  • Server-side validation of all uploads (file type, size, ownership)
  • Automatic scrubbing of authentication tokens and sensitive identifiers from error reports

No security measure is perfect. If we discover a security incident that affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant data protection authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law (within 72 hours under GDPR for high-risk incidents).
