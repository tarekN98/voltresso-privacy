Delete Your Account

Voltresso — developer: Tarek Sayed

You can request deletion of your Voltresso account and associated data at any time, in either of the two ways below.

How to Request Deletion

Option 1 — In the app: Open Voltresso, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, and confirm the prompt. Your account and profile data are deleted immediately upon confirmation.
Option 2 — By email: Send a request to voltresso@zohomail.eu from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." Requests submitted this way are processed within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

DataWhere it livesDeleted on request?
Profile (name, email, avatar, barista level)Voltresso servers (Supabase)Yes — permanently removed
Authentication credentialsVoltresso servers (Supabase Auth)Yes — revoked immediately
Brew logs, custom recipes, saved recipes, streaks/XPYour device only (local storage) — not synced to our serversNot applicable — we hold no copy. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes this permanently on your device.

Retention Period

Deletion is immediate. Voltresso does not run automated database backups, so once your account is deleted it is removed from our systems in full — there is no backup copy that persists afterward.

Note: Voltresso does not currently offer deletion of individual data types without deleting the full account. All account data is deleted as a single request.
